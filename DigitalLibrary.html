Digital Library

The reading room of the desk: digital libraries, wikis, and academic paper sites, free and paid, plus the brick-and-mortar libraries of Defiance and the counties around it. Project Gutenberg stands first, where it belongs.

Digital Libraries

  • Project Gutenberg - 70,000+ free public-domain ebooks to download or read online (Free)
  • Internet Archive - Massive digital library of books, movies, music, software, and archived websites (Free)
  • Open Library - Editable catalog of every book, with millions available to borrow digitally (Free)
  • HathiTrust - University-partnered repository of millions of scanned books and journals (Mixed: public domain free, more with member login)
  • Google Books - Search inside millions of books; full text for public-domain titles (Mixed: previews free, some titles for purchase)
  • Library of Congress Digital Collections - Photos, manuscripts, maps, recordings, and films from America's national library (Free)
  • Digital Public Library of America (DPLA) - One search portal across millions of items from US libraries and archives (Free)
  • Europeana - Cultural heritage from thousands of European museums, libraries, and archives (Free)
  • Biodiversity Heritage Library - The world's largest open-access library of biodiversity and natural-history literature (Free)
  • WorldCat - Search the combined catalogs of thousands of libraries worldwide at once (Free)
  • Chronicling America - 23M+ searchable pages of historic US newspapers, 1777-1963, from the Library of Congress (Free)
  • Smithsonian Open Access - Millions of CC0 images, 3D models, and data from all Smithsonian museums (Free)

Wikis & Reference

  • Wikipedia - The free encyclopedia anyone can edit; millions of articles in English (Free)
  • Wikisource - Free library of source texts: books, documents, speeches, and historical works (Free)
  • Wikimedia Commons - Repository of freely usable images, sounds, videos, and other media files (Free)
  • Wikibooks - Open-content textbooks and instructional books written collaboratively (Free)
  • Wikiversity - Free learning materials, courses, and research projects for all levels (Free)
  • Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy - Scholarly, peer-reviewed encyclopedia of philosophy, maintained by Stanford experts (Free)

Academic Papers & Journals

  • Google Scholar - Search engine indexing scholarly articles, theses, books, and patents across disciplines (Free: search free, some full texts paywalled)
  • DOAJ (Directory of Open Access Journals) - Directory indexing thousands of peer-reviewed open-access journals in all disciplines (Free)
  • CORE - Aggregates hundreds of millions of open-access research papers from repositories worldwide (Free)
  • BASE (Bielefeld Academic Search Engine) - Indexes 300M+ academic documents; roughly 60% available as free full text (Free)
  • OpenAlex - Open catalog of 250M+ scholarly works, authors, and institutions (Free)
  • Semantic Scholar - AI-powered academic search engine analyzing 200M+ papers and citations (Free)
  • arXiv - Preprint server for physics, math, computer science, and related fields (Free)
  • PubMed Central - NIH archive of full-text biomedical and life-sciences journal articles (Free)
  • ERIC - US Department of Education database of education research and literature (Free)
  • SSRN - Preprint network for social sciences, law, economics, and humanities research (Mixed: most papers free to download)
  • JSTOR - Deep archive of scholarly journals, books, and primary sources (Mixed: institutional subscription, some open content)
  • PhilPapers - Comprehensive index of philosophy publications with extensive open-access links (Mixed: index free, some full texts paywalled)
  • bioRxiv - Preprint server for biology and life-sciences research (Free)
  • medRxiv - Preprint server for medicine and health-sciences research (Free)
  • PLOS - Open-access publisher of peer-reviewed journals including PLOS ONE (Free)
  • NASA ADS (Astrophysics Data System) - Searchable database of astronomy and physics publications, hosted by Harvard/Smithsonian (Free)
  • ResearchGate - Professional network where 25M+ researchers share and discover publications (Free: account required)
  • Academia.edu - Platform for academics to share papers and follow research by topic (Mixed: free basic membership, paid Premium tier)
  • EBSCO - Research databases (EBSCOhost) covering journals, ebooks, and magazines for libraries (Mixed: paid for institutions, free through your library)
  • ProQuest - Research platform for journals, dissertations, newspapers, and ebooks (Mixed: paid for institutions, free through your library)

Free Books, Textbooks & Audio

  • Directory of Open Access Books (DOAB) - Discovery service for peer-reviewed academic books published under open-access licenses (Free)
  • OAPEN - Library of 40,000+ peer-reviewed open-access academic books, mainly humanities and social sciences (Free)
  • OpenStax - Free, peer-reviewed college textbooks from Rice University (Free)
  • MIT OpenCourseWare - Course materials from 2,500+ MIT classes: lectures, notes, assignments, exams (Free)
  • Standard Ebooks - Beautifully formatted, carefully proofread editions of public-domain classics (Free)
  • LibriVox - Volunteer-recorded audiobooks of public-domain works; 20,000+ recordings (Free)
  • Open Culture - Curated hub of free courses, movies, audiobooks, ebooks, and language lessons (Free)

Ohio & Your Library Card

  • Ohio Memory - 1.6M+ digitized Ohio photos, documents, and newspapers from 390+ institutions statewide (Free)
  • OhioLINK - Ohio's academic library consortium: shared catalog, e-journals, and Ohio theses and dissertations (Mixed: some collections public, borrowing needs member login)
  • The Ohio Digital Library - Borrow ebooks, audiobooks, and magazines from 179+ Ohio public libraries via OverDrive/Libby (Free with a member library card)
