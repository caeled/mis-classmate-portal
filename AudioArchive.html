Audio Archive

The listening and watching wing of the desk: sound effects by the tens of thousands, radio from its golden age, concerts taped by the people who stood in the crowd, and film that was never meant to survive. Free unless a tag says otherwise, and the Wayback Machine's own shelves are in here too, under the Internet Archive where they live.

Internet Archive: Audio

  • Internet Archive: Audio Archive Millions of recordings: music, audiobooks, news broadcasts, old time radio, podcasts. Free
  • Live Music Archive 170,000+ concert recordings from taper-friendly bands, the Grateful Dead above all. Free
  • The Great 78 Project Mass digitization of 78 rpm singles, 1880 to 1960: early jazz, blues, and voices otherwise lost. Free
  • Old-Time Radio Collection Golden-age radio from the cassette era, digitized: comedy, drama, mystery, variety. Free
  • LibriVox Public-domain audiobooks read by volunteers, chapter by chapter. Free

BBC & British Sound

  • BBC Sound Effects 33,000+ effects and field recordings from a century of BBC radio and TV. RemArc licence: personal, educational, research use. Free
  • BBC Sounds The BBC's radio home: live stations, drama, comedy, documentary, and the archive strands. Free
  • BBC Archive Curated clips and collections from the BBC vaults, TV and radio both. Free
  • British Library Sounds 50,000 recordings: music, drama, oral history, wildlife, accents and dialects. Some playback is region-limited. Mixed
  • British Pathé 85,000 newsreel films, 1896 to 1976, the filmed memory of the century. Free
  • BFI Replay Thousands of digitised films and TV programmes from the BFI National Archive. UK access. Free

Sound & Music Archives

  • Freesound A huge collaborative library of sounds and samples under Creative Commons licences. Free
  • Library of Congress: Audio Recordings The LOC's own audio portal across its divisions: recorded sound, folk life, poetry readings, interviews, and National Screening Room holdings. Free
  • National Jukebox Library of Congress: 10,000+ Victor recordings from 1901 to 1925, streaming free. Free
  • UCSB Cylinder Audio Archive 10,000+ Edison cylinder recordings, streamed and downloadable, the first decade of recorded sound. Free
  • Smithsonian Folkways The Smithsonian's record label: folk, world, spoken word, with liner notes as small books. Mixed
  • The Lomax Archive Alan Lomax's field recordings, 1946 into the 1990s: 17,400+ audio and video files. Free
  • The Global Jukebox Thousands of traditional songs from 1,000+ cultures, browsable on a world map. Free
  • Free Music Archive Curated free and legal music downloads, born at WFMU radio. Free
  • Musopen Royalty-free classical recordings and sheet music. Free
  • World Radio History A century of radio and broadcasting magazines and yearbooks, scanned whole. Free

Radio

  • Relic Radio Old time radio shows streaming by genre, a new episode always on. Free
  • RUSC R U Sitting Comfortably: golden-age radio drama and comedy, streamed. Free
  • OTRCAT The old time radio catalog: series guides, episode lists, and recordings. Mixed
  • REELRADIO The Reel Top 40 Radio Repository: airchecks from Top 40 radio's golden age, streaming since 1996. Mixed
  • Old Time Radio Researchers Group The collectors and scholars restoring golden-age series episode by episode, best sound, verified dates. Free
  • Digital Library of Amateur Radio & Communications Ham and shortwave history at the Internet Archive: QST, CQ and 73 magazines whole, plus recordings and club papers. Free
  • Radio Preservation Task Force The Library of Congress-backed effort to save America's radio heritage before the tapes give out. Free

Film, Video & TV News

  • American Archive of Public Broadcasting GBH and the Library of Congress preserving public radio and television; a third of it streams anywhere in the US, the rest at the LOC reading rooms. Mixed
  • Internet Archive: Moving Image Archive Feature films, newsreels, classic cartoons, and ephemeral film of every kind. Free
  • Prelinger Archives 60,000 advertising, educational, and industrial films: the raw quarry of remix culture. Free
  • TV News Archive Search and borrow from television news broadcasts, indexed by closed captions since 2009. Free
  • NASA Image and Video Library The space agency's own film and video vault, all public domain. Free
  • Vanderbilt Television News Archive Network evening news recorded since 1968; abstracts free, borrowing by arrangement. Mixed

Networks & Broadcast Vaults

  • NPR Public radio's own house: program archives reaching back toward All Things Considered's first broadcast in 1971, itself held in the National Recording Registry. Mixed
  • The Paley Center for Media 160,000 television and radio programs, the networks' memory kept by a museum. The catalog is searchable online; the listening happens on site in New York and Los Angeles. Mixed

The commercial networks keep licensing vaults rather than public archives. What of theirs can be heard freely mostly arrives through the doors above: the AAPB, Vanderbilt, and the Internet Archive.

Film Preservation

  • National Film Preservation Foundation Created by Congress to save America's orphan films; its grants have preserved films at 356 institutions, and preserved films stream on the site. Free
  • UCLA Film & Television Archive The second-largest moving image archive in the US after the Library of Congress, and the largest held by a university. Free
  • George Eastman Museum The world's oldest photography museum and one of its oldest film archives, home of the Selznick School of Film Preservation. Free
  • Academy Film Archive The Academy's own vault: preservation, restoration, and study of motion pictures. Free
  • The Film Foundation Founded by Martin Scorsese and fellow directors in 1990 to fund and fight for film preservation. Free

Social & Streaming

  • YouTube Everything, including more old radio and archive channels than any catalog can hold. Mixed
  • Vimeo Filmmakers' corner: shorts, documentaries, and careful work. Mixed
  • Dailymotion The other big video commons, strong in European archives and news. Mixed
  • SoundCloud Independent audio uploaded by its makers: demos, podcasts, experiments. Mixed
  • Bandcamp Artists selling and streaming their own records, liner notes included. Mixed
  • Mixcloud Long-form DJ mixes and radio shows, licensed for listening. Mixed
  • Twitch Live streams: games first, then music, talk, and everything else. Mixed
