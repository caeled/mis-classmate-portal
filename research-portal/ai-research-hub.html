A map of AI through an MIS lens

This is a broad, curated study guide to major model families and platforms, not an exhaustive registry of every checkpoint or fine-tune. It extends the Microsoft and Google deep dives with comparable provider profiles, primary research, specialist models, and practical evaluation. Catalog snapshots were reviewed on 3 November 2026; historical papers are labeled separately.

Choose a path: understand the research → inspect deployment options → evaluate a workflow.

ChatGPT and Claude are also product names; GPT and Claude model versions are inference choices. Microsoft Foundry, Vertex AI, and Amazon Bedrock are deployment platforms. Hugging Face hosts model artifacts and services. Ollama and vLLM run supported models. A comparison must identify which layer is being measured.

An eight-session learning sequence

  • Map an MIS task to inputs, outputs, stakeholders, and harms. Identify a non-AI baseline.
  • Read the Transformer and RAG papers; draw the difference between model training and retrieval.
  • Compare two provider profiles and trace claims to their catalogs.
  • Study an open model card, license, chat template, and hardware constraints.
  • Build a 20-case evaluation set with known evidence and deliberately missing answers.
  • Test a prompt using the library and score errors without knowing the model identity.
  • Design permissions, logging, fallback, and human review for a deployment.
  • Write a decision memo: observed benefits, costs, uncertainties, rejected alternatives, and next experiment.
