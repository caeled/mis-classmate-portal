Welcome to the MIS Research Portal

This portal supports Management Information Systems students, researchers, technology professionals, and lifelong learners. It combines traditional MIS governance with modern topics including cloud computing, artificial intelligence, enterprise architecture, cybersecurity, analytics, and digital transformation.

New to MIS? Start with the Launchpad

The MIS Launchpad is the starting page for students beginning an MIS program: a 7-day quick start, what MIS actually is, a semester-by-semester roadmap, 12 ready-made research topics, the journals that define the field, a skills lab for SQL, Excel/BI, and Python, career bands, and a simple workspace system. Portal Overview

The portal is organized into core operational research areas:

  • Cultural Differences – Global perspectives on MIS and technology adoption across Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa, and the Middle East.
  • Research Resources – Academic libraries, research databases, open-source repositories, and foundational deep learning papers.
  • Prompt Library – Model-specific prompt engineering, multi-model council workflows, and systems engineering harnesses.
  • Microsoft Ecosystem – Enterprise technology, Windows 11, Microsoft 365, Azure Cloud, and Copilot Studio.
  • Google Ecosystem – Google DeepMind & Brain foundations, Vertex AI, TPU silicon, NotebookLM, and Google Labs.
  • AI Ecosystem Analysis – Comparative evaluation of modern AI platforms through an MIS architectural lens.
  • Enterprise Architecture – Strategic alignment of business goals, applications, data, and technology infrastructure.
  • Digital Transformation Case Studies – Organizational modernization across manufacturing, healthcare, education, and government.
  • Security & Governance – Risk management, Zero Trust, NIST CSF, ISO 27001, COBIT, and AI safety rails.
  • Career Paths – Professional opportunities, certification maps, technical skill stacks, and leadership tracks.

Artificial Intelligence Research

The AI landscape changes rapidly. Understanding ecosystem strengths and architectural trade-offs is becoming an essential MIS skill.

  • Microsoft Copilot – Deep Microsoft 365 and Azure enterprise graph integration.
  • OpenAI ChatGPT – General reasoning, code generation, and custom GPT orchestration.
  • Anthropic Claude – Long-form document reasoning, policy analysis, and large context windows.
  • Google Gemini – Native multimodality, multi-million token context, and Google Cloud scale.
  • xAI Grok – Real-time discussion, contrarian debate, and live knowledge grounding.
  • Perplexity AI – Live citation discovery, academic source synthesis, and web index search.
