Why study this ecosystem?

Reasoning, coding, multimodal interfaces, and application tooling. This profile separates published model facts from suggested MIS experiments. The lab and research question are original teaching proposals, not benchmark results.

Dated catalog snapshot

The reviewed catalog lists GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, and GPT-6 Luna. It separates general models from image, realtime, transcription, and other specialized models. Treat model IDs and lifecycle status as version-specific.

Source reviewed 3 November 2026: official catalog / publisher. Availability, pricing, model aliases, and limits can change.

Model families and their jobs

GPT general models

Astra, Sol, Terra, and Luna occupy different capability and efficiency positions. Compare them on your actual workload instead of assuming one tier wins every task.

Specialist models

Image creation, realtime conversation, and transcription have distinct models and interfaces. A text-and-image input model does not automatically generate audio or images.

Historical lineage

GPT-4o and o1 in the original portal are historical examples; use the current catalog for available API targets.

Ecosystem layers

User experience

ChatGPT is a product surface; its tools, limits, and workspace configuration are separate from the raw model.

Developer platform

The model catalog links to Responses API, tool use, and SDK documentation. Application code controls evidence retrieval and actions.

Enterprise boundary

Direct OpenAI access and Microsoft-hosted deployments have separate configurations and contracts. Record the chosen service, not only the model name.

Suggested MIS lab

Compare policy-question answers from a larger and a cheaper candidate using the same 20 public excerpts. Include an unanswered question and conflicting revisions. Score support, abstention, and total cost per accepted answer. Test a deterministic keyword-search baseline too.

Deliverable: a one-page decision memo, an evidence table, a reproducible test set, and a log of unsuccessful cases. Use public or synthetic inputs. Record model version, host, settings, prompt, tools, runtime, and date.

Tradeoffs and failure modes

A successful fluent answer can hide a false citation. More reasoning effort is an experimental setting, not proof of correctness. Keep retrieval sources and tool results in the audit trail; validate structured output before using it downstream.

Before selecting a deployment, set acceptance thresholds for quality, latency, cost, and permissions. Test the actual workflow with realistic missing and conflicting data. A provider’s benchmark is a useful research lead, not a substitute for your evaluation.
