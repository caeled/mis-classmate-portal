Seminal AI & Deep Learning Research Papers

  • "Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017): Landmark Google Brain & Google Research paper introducing the Transformer architecture.
  • "AlphaFold 3: Accurate Structure Prediction of Biomolecular Interactions" (Nature / DeepMind, 2024): Atomistic-level modeling of DNA, RNA, ligands, and proteins.
  • "Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models" (Google DeepMind, 2023/2024): Native multimodal pre-training and long-context reasoning architectures.
  • "FlashAttention-2: Faster Attention with Better Parallelism" (Tri Dao, 2023): IO-aware exact self-attention optimizing GPU SRAM/HBM data movements.
  • "Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)" (Rafailov et al., 2023): Stable language model alignment without complex reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).

More wikis: structured knowledge and technical practice

  • Wikidata — Structured entities and references; useful for identifiers, knowledge graphs, and data-quality exercises.
  • Wikidata Query Service — Learn SPARQL and inspect how relationships support a query. Check statement references and missing values.
  • MediaWiki — Study the platform behind collaborative knowledge bases: revision history, templates, roles, and extensions.
  • ArchWiki — Community-maintained Linux administration reference. Commands are distribution-specific; check versions before use.
  • Wikimedia Commons — Find reusable media; inspect each file’s license and attribution requirements.
  • Wikisource — Digitized source texts; verify edition, transcription, and provenance against the original scan.

Wiki workflow: use an article to map vocabulary → inspect its references → open the underlying work → record a permanent revision link if citing the wiki itself. Talk pages and edit history reveal disputes; popularity is not evidence.

MIS literature, discovery, and citation tracing

  • AIS eLibrary — Information-systems journals and ICIS, AMCIS, and ECIS proceedings. Some full text requires membership or institutional access.
  • OpenAlex — Open scholarly discovery catalog; trace authors, institutions, topics, and related works.
  • Crossref Metadata Search — Resolve titles and DOIs; confirm publication metadata instead of accepting an AI-generated reference.
  • CORE — Search repository literature and open-access copies. Access could not be confirmed by the automated reader during this review.
  • Zotero documentation — Learn collections, tags, duplicate detection, annotations, and bibliography export.
  • Research Workbench — Search multiple discovery tools and keep an auditable search log.

Distinguish a discovery index, a publisher, a preprint server, an academic social network, and a citation manager. None guarantees that every item is peer reviewed. Check the work’s venue, version, corrections, and retractions.
