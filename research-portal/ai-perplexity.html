Why study this ecosystem?

Search and model orchestration for evidence discovery. This profile separates published model facts from suggested MIS experiments. The lab and research question are original teaching proposals, not benchmark results.

Ecosystem layers

Discovery

A question becomes queries and candidate sources.

Synthesis

Verification

The researcher opens the sources, checks attribution, and distinguishes evidence from inference.

Suggested MIS lab

Ask for sources on a narrow MIS adoption question. Open every citation and classify it as primary study, review, vendor claim, or commentary. Score whether each citation supports its attached sentence. Compare recall with an AIS eLibrary or Scholar search.

Deliverable: a one-page decision memo, an evidence table, a reproducible test set, and a log of unsuccessful cases. Use public or synthetic inputs. Record model version, host, settings, prompt, tools, runtime, and date.

Tradeoffs and failure modes

Citations are pointers, not a guarantee of correctness or peer review. A useful summary may still omit the most relevant study. Multiple pages repeating the same press release are not independent corroboration.

Before selecting a deployment, set acceptance thresholds for quality, latency, cost, and permissions. Test the actual workflow with realistic missing and conflicting data. A provider’s benchmark is a useful research lead, not a substitute for your evaluation.
