Why study this ecosystem?

Foundation models inside a cloud operating model. This profile separates published model facts from suggested MIS experiments. The lab and research question are original teaching proposals, not benchmark results.

Dated catalog snapshot

The opened Nova guide identifies itself as Version 1 and directs readers to Nova 2 documentation. Follow that version notice before selecting a release. Nova is Amazon’s model family; Bedrock is a managed service that also hosts models from other providers.

Source reviewed 3 November 2026: official catalog / publisher. Availability, pricing, model aliases, and limits can change.

Model families and their jobs

Understanding models

Select the exact model according to supported text, image, document, or video inputs.

Creative and speech models

Image, video, and speech workloads have different metering and evaluation criteria.

Third-party models in Bedrock

A Bedrock-hosted Claude or other model retains its own identity; it is not a Nova model.

Ecosystem layers

Cloud platform

Identity, monitoring, networking, and billing are part of the deployment design.

Managed model service

Model and region availability must be checked for the exact deployment.

Business workflow

Retrieval, guardrails, and orchestration surround the model; each has separate failure modes.

Suggested MIS lab

Design a public-document service desk with a search-only fallback. Draw the boundary between model hosting, the document store, authentication, and the approval workflow. Estimate the cost of model requests, retrieval, logs, and human review separately.

Deliverable: a one-page decision memo, an evidence table, a reproducible test set, and a log of unsuccessful cases. Use public or synthetic inputs. Record model version, host, settings, prompt, tools, runtime, and date.

Tradeoffs and failure modes

Using a managed cloud service does not transfer responsibility for data classification or application permissions. Cross-region routing and third-party model terms need explicit review.

Before selecting a deployment, set acceptance thresholds for quality, latency, cost, and permissions. Test the actual workflow with realistic missing and conflicting data. A provider’s benchmark is a useful research lead, not a substitute for your evaluation.
