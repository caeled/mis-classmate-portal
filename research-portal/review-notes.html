Research edition · 3 November 2026 · Original student exercises are labeled as such.

What the original archive contained

Eleven HTML pages and one shared stylesheet, duplicated identically under copilot/. The portal already covered Microsoft, Google, AI comparisons, MIS careers, culture, architecture, case studies, security, resources, and prompts. The original sections and their links are retained. Both deployment paths receive the same additions.

What this edition adds

  • All 11 original pages gain contextual resources or practical study material.
  • A research workbench adds external-search launchers, wiki source tracing, and an evidence-matrix workflow.
  • The prompt library gains 16 templates with required inputs, copy buttons, filtering, and observable checks.
  • An AI hub connects 11 new provider profiles with the existing Microsoft and Google deep dives.
  • Additional guides cover seven open/emerging model families and platforms, specialist AI, 17 annotated papers, and a model evaluation lab.
  • A local cost estimator and downloadable TSV worksheet support comparisons without API keys or a backend.

Important qualifications to the original material

  • The four original case studies include precise but unattributed outcome figures. They are preserved as illustrative, unverified scenario inputs.
  • Historical model names, context limits, product labels, and certification versions are preserved. Use dated catalogs and issuer documentation before relying on them.
  • A provider is not a fixed personality. Prompt style, model version, tools, and application design influence behavior.
  • RAG, citations, model councils, and provenance metadata do not guarantee truth or privacy.
  • Open weights and open-source licensing are different questions; inspect each model license.
  • The original gdpr.eu link is not the official EU legal portal. The added section points to EUR-Lex and distinguishes general summaries from legal obligations.
  • National cultural summaries are hypotheses for investigation, not reliable descriptions of individuals.

Source and scope policy

New provider snapshots use official catalogs, documentation, or verified publisher pages reviewed on 3 November 2026. Research links point to original papers and technical reports. Exercises, decision questions, and evaluation rubrics are original teaching suggestions. No proprietary model benchmarking was performed for this edition.

This is a curated breadth-first guide, not a claim to enumerate every AI model. Do not interpret an unlisted model as unavailable. Prices and numeric token limits are intentionally not frozen into a comparison table; use the linked catalog and the estimator with current rates.

Automated retrieval could not read every site: Meta’s main model page was unavailable, so the verified publisher collection was used; CORE, ArchWiki, and GAO returned access restrictions; EUR-Lex presented a browser verification page; HELM exposed no readable text. Those destinations are linked as research resources without claiming their full contents were audited. Original external links are retained and are not all revalidated.

Maintenance and deployment

Unzip the archive into a static hosting directory so index.html is at its root. Preserve relative filenames and the copilot/ compatibility folder. No build step, package installation, API key, server database, or account is required for the portal itself. External resources have their own access requirements.

Review model catalogs and version notices regularly. Add a review date to future changes. Keep the two paths synchronized when editing. Read README-DEPLOY.txt and VALIDATION.txt for packaging and checks.
