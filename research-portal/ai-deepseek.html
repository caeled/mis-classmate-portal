Why study this ecosystem?

Reasoning research, mixture-of-experts models, and deployment choices. This profile separates published model facts from suggested MIS experiments. The lab and research question are original teaching proposals, not benchmark results.

Local serving

Suggested MIS lab

Compare two settings on synthetic inventory-planning problems with known arithmetic answers. Record accuracy, output length, latency, and cost. Then add ambiguous demand data and check whether the model states the missing assumptions instead of fabricating values.

Deliverable: a one-page decision memo, an evidence table, a reproducible test set, and a log of unsuccessful cases. Use public or synthetic inputs. Record model version, host, settings, prompt, tools, runtime, and date.

Tradeoffs and failure modes

Mixture-of-experts active parameter counts are not the same as total model storage. A small distilled model is not a free equivalent of its larger teacher. Report runtime and precision alongside the model name.

Before selecting a deployment, set acceptance thresholds for quality, latency, cost, and permissions. Test the actual workflow with realistic missing and conflicting data. A provider’s benchmark is a useful research lead, not a substitute for your evaluation.
