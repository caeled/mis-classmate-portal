Match the model to the information problem

A university research assistant may combine OCR, embeddings, retrieval, reranking, a language model, and speech. Diagnose each stage before replacing the whole system. The following are research starting points, not claims that one provider leads every category.

Embeddings and rerankers

Represent documents for retrieval and reorder candidates by relevance. Evaluate retrieval before generation.

Measure: Recall at k, ranking quality, language coverage, and permission filtering.

  • Cohere models
  • Mistral models

OCR and document understanding

Extract characters, layout, tables, and fields. A clean text output can still transpose digits or lose row structure.

Measure: Exact-field match, table structure, missing-value behavior, and source-region traceability.

  • Mistral OCR catalog
  • IBM Granite Vision

Speech recognition and voice

Separate transcription, speaker attribution, text-to-speech, and real-time conversation. Each has a different failure pattern.

Measure: Word error rate, speaker attribution, interruption behavior, latency, and proper-name accuracy.

  • OpenAI audio catalog
  • Mistral Voxtral catalog
  • Google model catalog

Image generation and editing

Compare instruction adherence, typography, preservation of reference details, and edit locality. Generation models differ from image-understanding models.

Measure: A blind rubric for the requested scene or edit, plus provenance and usage-rights checks.

  • Black Forest Labs / FLUX
  • Stability AI
  • OpenAI image catalog

Video and audio generation

Treat temporal consistency, motion, synchronization, and controllability as distinct dimensions. A good frame does not guarantee a coherent clip.

Measure: Continuity, prompt adherence, audio alignment, revision effort, and cost per accepted clip.

  • Google DeepMind models
  • xAI Imagine
  • Black Forest Labs

Forecasting and structured business data

Not every MIS problem is language generation. Forecasting, classification, regression, and optimization often need structured models and explicit assumptions.

Measure: Time-based validation, leakage checks, appropriate baselines, calibration, and operational cost.

  • Granite time-series family
  • scikit-learn documentation

Scientific models

Domain models such as AlphaFold address a particular scientific problem. They should not be compared to general chat using conversational preference.

Measure: Domain-specific validation, uncertainty, experimental confirmation, and intended-use boundaries.

  • AlphaFold 3 paper
  • Google DeepMind research

A useful multimodal capstone

Use a public annual report containing narrative, tables, and charts. Build a provenance map from page → extracted fact → calculation → summary. Insert one deliberately unanswerable question. Compare direct multimodal reading with OCR plus retrieval. Report which stage introduced each error and how human review caught it.
