Read papers as evidence

Dates below are first-release years. These links point to research records, often preprints or technical reports; an arXiv entry alone does not establish peer review. Inspect the version history, venue, code, data, baselines, and limitations. A historical result is not a current product specification.

Reading order: Transformer → RAG → instruction tuning → evaluation → a provider report. For each, write the problem, method, evidence, limitation, and one implication for an MIS deployment.

Attention Is All You Need

Introduces the Transformer architecture based on attention.

Study question: How does the architecture differ from retrieval or persistent application memory?
