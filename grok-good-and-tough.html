Good & Tough.

Generate sharper, more demanding prompts for competency evidence, architecture trade-offs, and real-world claims. Built for pressure-testing, not encouragement.

Tough Assessor Generator

Pick a focus and claim. The output is a ready-to-paste prompt that expects you to defend assumptions, surface trade-offs, and name frameworks without fluff.

How to use it

  • Paste the generated prompt into Grok (or any model) along with your draft evidence or notes.
  • Answer the questions it produces. The point is to surface weak assumptions before an assessor does.
  • Log the session in the main portal’s AI-use log if your course requires disclosure.
  • Export notes from the study areas or toolkit as backup; this page does not store anything.

This page is a tool, not a grade. Your course rubric and assessor still decide. Use it to raise the floor on your own work.
